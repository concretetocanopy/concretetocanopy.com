Each route is hand-crafted to reveal a different face of Toronto's hidden landscape — from ancient creek beds to gritty urban edges.

Ravine

Don Valley Brickworks

A short walk through the Rosedale neighbourhood descending 60 meters below street level for a 2 hour walk into the Don valley with a unique view of downtown Toronto. We will pass through the Brickworks historical site, leading to a spot for impressive unique views of the core after leaving the canopy of a mature forest. Defintiely worth the ascent to the look out.

⏱ 2 hours 📍 Meeting point Outside Castle Frank Subway on Line 2 👥 Max 8 people

$50.00 per person
